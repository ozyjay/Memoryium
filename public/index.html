A little guide to playing

  1. Tap or click a card to see its picture.
  2. Choose another card. Do the pictures match?
  3. A matching pair stays face up. If they’re different, take a look, then choose “Turn these cards back”.
  4. Keep going until you’ve found every pair.

You can show all the pictures whenever you like. Use Tab and Enter to play with a keyboard.
